What Keel does

Everything a manager needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

Teams, tasks and people in one calm place — built for someone who runs more than one team at a time.

Teams

Every team has one leader, its members and one main task — with progress shown at a glance.

Tasks

Due dates, priorities, notes and sub-todos. A task can belong to a team, to people, to both, or stand alone.

People

Every person keeps unlimited phones and emails, a role, notes and a photo. Call, email or message straight from their card.

Multiple organizations

Keep work, community and side projects apart — each organization has its own teams, tasks and people.

Private by design

No account, no server, no internet. Your teams, tasks and people never leave your phone.

Backup you control

Export everything to a file you own and restore it anywhere. Your data, your storage, your rules.
