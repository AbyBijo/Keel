One manager. Many teams. Everything stays on your phone.

Keel is an offline organization manager for Android, built for one person running an organization with many people, teams and tasks.

No account. No login. No server. No internet. No ads. No tracking. Everything you enter lives on your phone — and nowhere else.

## What's new in 1.3.0

**Haptics that actually buzz** — vibration now drives the motor directly, so it works whether or not the phone's touch-vibration setting is on, and Light, Medium and Heavy finally feel different. New "Test vibration" button in Settings, and haptics on more taps — checkboxes, saves and deletes.

**Notifications that remind you** — the morning digest now refreshes its counts, and per-task reminders fire on the due date and when a task goes overdue, on a high-importance channel so they show and buzz.

**Home scroll, fixed** — the team strip can no longer steal a vertical swipe, and the tab bar blur is lighter, so scrolling stays smooth.

**Teams + People, one tab — and Calendar** — Teams and People now live in one tab with Teams | People sub-tabs, and Calendar takes People's old spot: a month view of task due dates, dots for open tasks (red when overdue), tap a day to see its tasks, add one due that day, or keep a note for the day. A task you tick done on a day stays on that day, under the open tasks, shown as done — and clears after about a day.

**Editing, the proper way** — editing a team or a person now opens the same full slide-up sheet as creating one, pre-filled, with everything changeable.

**Task cards + Up next** — a task card shows only the next sub-todo to do, with the done ones as a count ("2 of 8 done"). A completed task no longer vanishes from Up next — it drops to the bottom, shown as done, and clears after about a day.

**Organizations** — creating one now asks for its type (Studio, Company, Club, Family or Other) in a clean slide-up sheet, and the org list is now one grouped card with the organizations as rows inside it.

## What's in this release

- **Teams** — create a team with a leader, members, a colour and a main task, and see each team's progress at a glance.
- **People** — every person keeps unlimited phone numbers and email addresses, a role, notes and a photo. Call, email or message (WhatsApp) straight from their card.
- **Tasks** — due dates, priorities, notes, sub-todos and assignees. A task can belong to a team, to people, to both, or stand alone.
- **Home, at a glance** — a progress dial, an Up next list, stat tiles, team cards and global search.
- **Backup** — export everything to a file you control and restore it later, through the Android file picker.
- **Made yours** — dark mode, text size, accent colour, haptics with vibration intensity, and week start, all in Settings → Personalisation.

## Download

- **Most modern Android phones:** `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`
- **Older 32-bit phones:** `app-armeabi-v7a-release.apk`
- **x86_64 devices (mostly emulators):** `app-x86_64-release.apk`
- **Debug build (for testing):** `app-debug.apk`

(Sizes vary with each version — GitHub shows the size next to every file on the release page.)

## Privacy

No `INTERNET` permission. No analytics, no trackers, no account, no ads. Photos stay in the app's private storage; the database stores only the path.
