One manager. Many teams. Everything stays on your phone.

Keel is an offline organization manager for Android, built for one person running an organization with many people, teams and tasks.

No account. No login. No server. No internet. No ads. No tracking. Everything you enter lives on your phone — and nowhere else.

## What's in this release

- **Teams** — create a team with a leader, members, a colour and a main task, and see each team's progress at a glance.
- **People** — every person keeps unlimited phone numbers and email addresses, a role, notes and a photo. Call, email or message (WhatsApp) straight from their card.
- **Tasks** — due dates, priorities, notes, sub-todos and assignees. A task can belong to a team, to people, to both, or stand alone.
- **Home, at a glance** — a progress dial, an Up next list, stat tiles, team cards and global search.
- **Backup** — export everything to a file you control and restore it later, through the Android file picker.
- **Made yours** — dark mode, text size, accent colour, haptics with vibration intensity, and week start, all in Settings → Personalisation.

## Download

- **Most modern Android phones:** `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`
- **Older 32-bit phones:** `app-armeabi-v7a-release.apk`
- **x86_64 devices (mostly emulators):** `app-x86_64-release.apk`
- **Debug build (for testing):** `app-debug.apk`

(Sizes vary with each version — GitHub shows the size next to every file on the release page.)

## Privacy

No `INTERNET` permission. No analytics, no trackers, no account, no ads. Photos stay in the app's private storage; the database stores only the path.
